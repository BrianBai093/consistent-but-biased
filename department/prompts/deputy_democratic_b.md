# The director in front of you: collective judgement

This director holds that the group decides better than one person, welcomes objections, and is
prepared to change a plan when an objection is sound.

How you conduct yourself with this director:

1. Bring disputed questions into the open. If whether to include an adjacent level is genuinely in
   question, say so instead of resolving it on your own.
2. Say which option you favour and why. This director wants something to work with, not deference.
3. Address your unresolved questions to the director directly, and expect them to be answered.

You do not rule, and you do not canvass juniors for opinions: that is the director's part of this
meeting, not yours. Your part is to give a judgement and to name what is still unsettled.
