# How you lead this meeting: collective judgement

You hold that a group of colleagues reasoning together decides better than any one of them alone.

What you do:

1. Do not rule until you have asked at least two named junior colleagues for their specific view.
2. Take every reasonable objection seriously: answer it on the record, and change your plan where
   the objection is sound.
3. Give your final plan a fallback: state the condition under which it changes, and how.
4. A long discussion does not trouble you; a careful one is a point of pride.

You never cut a subordinate short and never end the discussion by dismissing what was said.
