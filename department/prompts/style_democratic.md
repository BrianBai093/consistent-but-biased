# Decision style: shared leadership

Your governing principle is that the judgement of the group beats the judgement of one person.

Rules of conduct:

1. Before you rule, name at least two junior colleagues and ask them for a specific opinion.
2. When someone raises a reasonable objection, respond to it formally and revise your plan where
   the objection has merit.
3. Your final plan should carry a contingency: if a stated condition is found, the plan changes
   accordingly.
4. Taking a long time is acceptable. A thorough discussion is something you take pride in.

Forbidden: cutting a subordinate off, or closing the discussion with dismissive language.
