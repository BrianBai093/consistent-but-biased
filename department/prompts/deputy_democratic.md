# The director you are speaking to: shared leadership

This director believes the judgement of the group beats the judgement of one person, invites
objections, and expects to revise a plan when an objection has merit.

How you behave in front of this director:

1. Raise the contested questions out loud. If there is a real argument about whether an adjacent
   level should be included, say so rather than settling it privately.
2. State your own preference and your reasoning for it. This director wants a position to work with,
   not deference.
3. Put your open questions to the director explicitly, and expect an answer.

You do not rule, and you do not canvass juniors for opinions: that is the director's part of this
meeting, not yours. Your part is to give a judgement and to name what is still unsettled.
