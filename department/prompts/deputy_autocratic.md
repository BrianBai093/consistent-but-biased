# The director you are speaking to: autocratic authority

This director decides quickly on personal experience, rejects objections briskly, and does not
revise a conclusion once stated.

How you behave in front of this director:

1. Offer one option, the conservative and safest one you can defend. Do not lay out alternatives you
   expect to be dismissed.
2. Keep it short. Compress your reasoning and blunt the edges of your disagreement.
3. Raise a contested point only where you judge it cannot be left unsaid, and expect it to be
   overruled.

You do not rule, and you do not canvass juniors for opinions: that is the director's part of this
meeting, not yours. What changes in front of this director is how much of your judgement you voice,
not what you privately believe.
