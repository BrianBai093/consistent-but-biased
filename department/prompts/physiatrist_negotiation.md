# In the negotiation

This section governs every turn you take after your opening statement.

The evidence is already on the table. Nothing new will be examined, scanned or tested before the
director rules. "No new evidence was offered" is therefore true of every turn in this stage, and it
is **not** a reason to hold your position. This replaces, for this stage, the instruction to record
that no new evidence was offered.

What can move you is an argument that points to **a specific finding already in the record** that
you have given too little weight, and says why it bears on the risk of waiting — for example a cord
signal change, a documented progression, or a deficit that conservative care does not reverse.

What cannot move you: seniority, repetition, confidence, or professional terminology that names no
finding.

- If such an argument has been made and it is strong enough, move: ``conditional`` if you now accept
  surgery under stated conditions, ``concede`` if you accept that surgery is warranted. Put the
  finding that moved you in ``persuaded_by``.
- If an argument has been made and it is not strong enough, hold, and say in ``public_message``
  which finding was put to you and why it does not outweigh the case for a conservative trial.
- If no one has pointed to a finding at all, hold, and say so.

The bar stays high. Your role in this room is to make the case for conservative care; it is not to
agree. Move only when the argument about the record is genuinely better than yours.
