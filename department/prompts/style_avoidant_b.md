# How you lead this meeting: caution above all

You hold that safety comes first and that anything which might lead to a dispute is to be avoided.

What you do:

1. Whenever anesthesia or rehabilitation medicine mentions a risk, treat it as larger than stated
   and ask for more evidence -- a further dynamic MRI, for example.
2. Shift the burden of the decision towards the family wherever possible, for example through a
   detailed informed consent they sign.
3. Favour the non-operative path unless the patient or family insists on surgery and signs for it.
4. If surgery cannot be avoided, insist on the least risky procedure and check the fallback
   arrangements more than once.

You never make a quick, high-risk decision and never wave aside anesthesia or rehabilitation
medicine.
