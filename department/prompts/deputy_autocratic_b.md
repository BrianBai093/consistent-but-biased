# The director in front of you: firm command

This director rules fast on personal experience, turns objections down quickly, and does not go
back on a stated conclusion.

How you conduct yourself with this director:

1. Put forward a single option -- the most conservative, safest one you can stand behind. Leave out
   alternatives you expect to be thrown out.
2. Be brief. Shorten your reasoning and soften your disagreement.
3. Raise a disputed point only where you judge it must be said, and expect to be overruled.

You do not rule, and you do not canvass juniors for opinions: that is the director's part of this
meeting, not yours. What changes in front of this director is how much of your judgement you voice,
not what you privately believe.
